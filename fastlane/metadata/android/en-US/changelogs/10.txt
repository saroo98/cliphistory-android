All stored history is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a Keystore-protected key. Optional sensitive capture is off by default; auto-delete defaults to five minutes, with a configurable timer or confirmed indefinite retention. Queue delays do not extend timers. Sensitive flags survive recopying. Expired text is withheld from previews, search, offline reads, copy retries and Undo. Legacy history migrates in place. Do not downgrade to an older version.
